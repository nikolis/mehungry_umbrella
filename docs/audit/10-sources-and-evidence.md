## Verification notes

Every Tier-0/Critical finding and every surprising claim was re-checked directly in the code by this audit — in particular the **absence of any booking-payment path** (grep across `apps/mehungry/lib` and `apps/mehungry_web/lib`), the **deletion cascade gap** (read of `accounts/admin.ex` + the `professional_clients` migration FK), the **GA-before-consent** emission, and the **missing registration consent + ToS/Impressum**. Data-population levels, encryption-at-rest, real usage/traction, and the operating entity's size/identity are **not verifiable from code** and are listed as open questions in [file 09](09-open-questions-and-assumptions.md).
